Our story

Made to be worn, not just owned

Trenza Elegance began as a small atelier working with a handful of weavers who cared as much about the drape of a fabric as its colour. Every piece is still cut in small batches, so what you order rarely looks the same twice.

We keep the palette quiet on purpose — blush, clay, and undyed cotton — so the pieces mix easily and last beyond a season.
